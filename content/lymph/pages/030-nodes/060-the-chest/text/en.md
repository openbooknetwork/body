Lymph from the lungs flows towards the root of each lung, through [nodes](@lung-nodes) inside the lung, where the [right](@right-main-bronchus) and [left main bronchus](@left-main-bronchus) enter it, and under the fork of the [trachea](@trachea). From there it climbs through the [nodes between the lungs](@chest-nodes), beside the windpipe, towards the venous angles.

@image tracheobronchial-nodes-gray-2d175a

Macrophages in the lungs swallow inhaled dust and soot and carry it to these nodes, where it stays for life. In city dwellers and smokers the nodes turn grey or black. A lung cancer spreads along the same route, and which of these nodes hold tumour decides how it is treated.
