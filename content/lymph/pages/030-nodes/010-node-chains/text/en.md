An adult has several hundred lymph nodes, and nearly all lymph passes through at least one before it reaches the blood. The nodes gather in chains along the large vessels: in the [neck](@neck-nodes) beside the jugular veins, in the [armpit](@axillary-nodes), in the [groin](@inguinal-nodes), along the [aorta](@aortic-nodes) and in the [mesentery](@mesenteric-nodes) of the gut.

In the neck, the armpit and the groin the nodes lie close under the skin, which is why a doctor feels there for swollen ones. A healthy node is soft and smaller than a centimetre. A node that turns hard and fixed can mean a cancer that has spread to it.
