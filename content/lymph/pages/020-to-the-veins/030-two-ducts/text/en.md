The [thoracic duct](@thoracic-duct) empties at the [left venous angle](@left-venous-angle). On the right, a [right lymphatic duct](@right-lymphatic-duct) about a centimetre long, often split into separate trunks, ends at the [right venous angle](@right-venous-angle). It drains the right side of the head and neck, the right arm and the right half of the chest, one quarter of the body.

@image right-lymph-trunks-gray-582165

From the angles the lymph flows on with the venous blood through the [brachiocephalic veins](@brachiocephalic-veins) and the [superior vena cava](@superior-vena-cava) into the right atrium. The pressure in these veins is close to zero, which lets the lymph drain in.
