The [heart](@heart) sits in the middle of the chest, behind the breastbone and between the two [lungs](@lungs), with its tip pointing down and to the left. It is about the size of a fist and weighs some 300 grams.

@image heart-front-gray-97457a

At rest it beats about 70 times a minute, some 100,000 times a day, and each beat sends about 70 millilitres of blood on its way.

The body holds about five litres of blood. At rest the heart pumps roughly that volume every minute, so the whole of the blood goes round about once a minute. In hard exercise the output rises four times or more.
