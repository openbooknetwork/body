The [heart](@heart) sits in the middle of the chest, behind the breastbone and between the two lungs, with its tip pointing down and to the left. It is about the size of its owner's fist and weighs some 300 grams. At rest it beats about 70 times a minute, 100,000 times a day, and every beat sends about 70 millilitres of blood on its way.

@image heart-front-gray-97457a

The body holds about five litres of blood. Each minute at rest the heart pumps roughly that volume, so the whole of the blood goes round once a minute. In hard exercise the output rises four or five times.
