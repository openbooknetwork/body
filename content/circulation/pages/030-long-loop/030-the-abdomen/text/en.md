Below the diaphragm the aorta gives off three unpaired branches to the gut and one pair to the kidneys. The [celiac trunk](@celiac-trunk), a short thick stem, feeds the stomach, the liver and the spleen. The [superior mesenteric artery](@superior-mesenteric-artery) feeds the whole of the small intestine and the first half of the large, and the [inferior mesenteric](@inferior-mesenteric-artery) the rest of the colon.

The two [renal arteries](@renal-arteries) are short and wide. The kidneys weigh 300 grams between them and take a fifth of the heart's output, about a litre a minute, because their work is to filter the blood: 180 litres of filtrate a day, of which all but a litre or two goes back.
