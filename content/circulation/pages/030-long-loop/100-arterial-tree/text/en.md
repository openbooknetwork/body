Estimates for all the vessels of one body laid end to end run to about 100,000 kilometres, and nearly all of that length is capillaries too fine to see. The model shows the [arterial tree](@arteries) down to branches a few millimetres wide, each generation narrower and more numerous than the last.

@image arterial-system-7dbf4d

At any moment the arteries hold only about an eighth of the blood. Their walls are thick and muscular, and the smallest, the arterioles, can narrow or widen on signals from nerves and hormones.

@image artery-vein-d8f4d4

This is how the body sends more blood to the muscles in a run and to the gut after a meal, and how it sets the blood pressure as a whole.
