The two [renal arteries](@renal-arteries) leave the aorta just below the superior mesenteric artery, short and wide. At each kidney they divide, and their [branches](@intrarenal-arteries) fan out inside it until they reach about a million filters on each side, the glomeruli.

@image glomerulus-gray-895106

The kidneys weigh about 300 grams together and receive about a fifth of the heart's output, some 1.1 litres a minute. Their work is to filter the blood: about 180 litres of fluid a day pass into the tubules, and all but about a litre and a half is taken back.

@image nephron-vessels-gray-273205

The blood gathers in the [veins of the kidney](@intrarenal-veins) and leaves by the [renal veins](@renal-veins) into the inferior vena cava. The left renal vein is the longer, since the vena cava lies to the right of the midline, and it crosses in front of the aorta just below the superior mesenteric artery.
