The [rectum](@rectum), the last 12 cm or so of the large intestine, follows the curve of the sacrum into the pelvis and ends at the anal canal, about 4 cm long. It is usually empty. A mass movement from the [descending colon](@descending-colon) fills it, and the stretch of its wall is felt as the urge to empty the bowel.

@image sigmoid-rectum-gray-305bd3

The stretch also starts a reflex. Waves of contraction run down the sigmoid colon and rectum, and the inner ring of muscle round the anal canal relaxes. Nerve endings in the lining of the canal can tell gas from liquid or solid, so a person can let out wind and hold the rest.
