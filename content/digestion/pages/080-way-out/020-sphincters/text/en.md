Two rings of muscle close the anal canal. The inner one, the thickened end of the gut's smooth muscle, stays contracted without thought. The [external anal sphincter](@external-anal-sphincter) round it is skeletal muscle under voluntary control. Above it the [puborectalis](@puborectalis) loops behind the gut and pulls it towards the pubic bone, bending the passage at a sharp angle.

When the time is right, the external sphincter and puborectalis relax, the angle straightens and the abdominal muscles push to empty the [rectum](@rectum). When it is not, they tighten, and the urge fades after a few minutes, coming back hours later or when more faeces arrive.
