The [tongue](@tongue) is a mass of muscle covered with a moist lining. Muscles inside it change its shape, and muscles from the jaw and the hyoid bone move it. While you chew it pushes food between the teeth and mixes it with saliva into a soft lump, the bolus. Then it pushes the bolus back into the throat.

@image tongue-muscles-gray-9a9f95

Its upper surface is rough with small projections, the papillae. Most taste buds sit on them, some 3,000 to 10,000 in an adult, each a cluster of about 50 cells.

@image taste-buds-gray-aec335

Taste buds tell five tastes: sweet, sour, salty, bitter and umami, the taste of meat broth. Most of what we call flavour comes from smell, which is why food tastes flat with a blocked nose.
