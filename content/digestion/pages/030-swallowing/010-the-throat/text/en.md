Behind the mouth the food path crosses the airway. The [pharynx](@pharynx), a funnel of muscle 12 to 14 cm long, leads from behind the nose down to the esophagus, and air passes through it on its way to the [larynx](@larynx). Once the tongue pushes a bolus back into it, swallowing runs as a reflex that cannot be stopped halfway.

@image head-section-gray-544907

In about a second the [soft palate](@soft-palate) rises to close off the nose, the larynx is pulled up and forward under the tongue, and the [epiglottis](@epiglottis) tips back over its entrance. Breathing stops for that moment. Then the muscle of the pharynx squeezes from the top down and pushes the bolus into the esophagus.
