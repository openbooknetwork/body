The [duodenum](@duodenum), the first 25 cm of small intestine, curves round the head of the [pancreas](@pancreas). Its name comes from the Latin for twelve, its length in finger breadths. In its descending part the [bile duct](@bile-duct) and the [pancreatic duct](@pancreatic-duct) usually join and open on a small mound, the major duodenal papilla, guarded by a ring of muscle.

Chyme arrives from the stomach at a pH of about 2. When acid touches the duodenal lining, its cells release secretin into the blood, and the pancreas answers with a juice rich in bicarbonate that neutralises the acid. Secretin, found by William Bayliss and Ernest Starling in 1902, was the first hormone to be discovered.
