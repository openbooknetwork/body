The large intestine is about 1.5 m long and frames the small intestine on three sides. It starts at the [cecum](@cecum) in the lower right, climbs as the [ascending colon](@ascending-colon), crosses under the stomach as the [transverse colon](@transverse-colon) and runs down the left side as the [descending colon](@descending-colon). An S-shaped loop, the sigmoid colon, then leads into the [rectum](@rectum).

About 1.5 litres of watery chyme cross from the ileum each day. The colon takes back most of the water and salt, so that less than a tenth of a litre leaves in the faeces. Its contents move slowly, and a few times a day, often after breakfast, a strong mass movement pushes them a long way at once.
