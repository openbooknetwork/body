Along most of the colon the outer layer of muscle is gathered into three flat bands, the [taeniae coli](@taeniae-coli). They start together at the base of the [appendix](@appendix), which surgeons find by following them, and spread out again over the rectum. The bands are shorter than the tube, so they pucker its wall into a row of pouches, the haustra.

@image teniae-coli-openstax-1f4354

The ring muscle squeezes a few pouches at a time. Each contraction builds over about 30 seconds and fades over the next minute, rolling the contents against the wall so that water and salt can be absorbed. Small bags of fat hang from the outer surface along the bands.
