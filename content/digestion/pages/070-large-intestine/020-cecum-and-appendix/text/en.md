The [ileum](@ileum) joins the large intestine from the side, through the ileocecal valve. Below the junction the [cecum](@cecum) forms a blind pouch about 6 cm deep in the lower right abdomen. The valve and a thickened ring of muscle let chyme pass into the colon and keep the colon's contents, rich in bacteria, from flowing back.

The [appendix](@appendix) opens from the cecum about 2 cm below the valve. It is a narrow tube, on average about 9 cm long, with much lymphoid tissue in its wall.

@image appendix-section-gray-96ca48

When its opening is blocked it can swell and become infected. The pain of appendicitis often starts around the navel and moves to the lower right. Some 7 to 9 percent of people have it in their lifetime.
