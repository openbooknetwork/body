The brainstem joins the brain to the cord. From top to bottom it has three parts. The [midbrain](@midbrain) carries the [cerebral peduncles](@cerebral-peduncles), the stalks that bring the great motor tracts down from the hemispheres. The [pons](@pons) bulges in front of the cerebellum. The [medulla oblongata](@medulla-oblongata) narrows into the spinal cord at the base of the skull.

Ten of the twelve pairs of cranial nerves, from the oculomotor to the hypoglossal, leave the brainstem, and their nuclei lie inside it. The medulla holds the centres that set the rhythm of breathing and the pressure of the blood. This is why an injury to the medulla can stop breathing.
