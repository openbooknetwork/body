A command to move a finger starts in the [precentral gyrus](@left-precentral-gyrus). The fibres of the pyramidal tract run down through the [internal capsule](@internal-capsules), the [cerebral peduncles](@cerebral-peduncles) of the midbrain and the [pons](@pons), and gather in the pyramids on the front of the [medulla](@medulla-oblongata). There most cross to the other side and run down the cord in the [corticospinal tracts](@corticospinal-tracts).

@image motor-tract-gray-b724f3

Each pyramid carries about a million fibres, and the thickest conduct at about 70 metres a second. In the cord the fibres end on motor neurons, whose axons run out to the muscles. The axon of a motor neuron at the lower end of the cord reaches the foot, about a metre away.
