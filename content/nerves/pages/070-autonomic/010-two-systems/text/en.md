A second set of nerves runs the organs without our asking. Its sympathetic half leaves the cord between the first thoracic and second lumbar segments and relays in two [chains of ganglia](@sympathetic-trunks) beside the spine. Readying the body for effort, it speeds the [heart](@heart), widens airways and pupils, pulls sugar from the [liver](@liver) and makes the [adrenal glands](@adrenal-glands) release adrenaline.

The parasympathetic half leaves the brainstem in four cranial nerves and the cord in its sacral segments. It slows the heart, drives digestion in the [stomach](@stomach) and gut, and empties the [urinary bladder](@urinary-bladder). Most organs receive both halves, and the balance between them sets how fast the heart beats or the gut moves.
