The [vagus nerve](@vagus-nerves), the tenth cranial nerve, leaves the [medulla](@medulla-oblongata) and wanders further than any other, which gave it its Latin name. It runs down the neck beside the carotid artery, through the chest past the [heart](@heart) and lungs, and along the oesophagus to the [stomach](@stomach) and the gut as far as the colon.

@image vagus-gray-e92d5d

About three quarters of all parasympathetic fibres run in the two vagus nerves. Most of the vagus fibres carry news the other way, about four in five: they tell the brainstem how stretched, how acid or how full the organs are.

In 1921 Otto Loewi stimulated the vagus of a frog heart and moved the fluid around it to a second heart, which slowed as well. The nerve had released a chemical, later identified as acetylcholine.
