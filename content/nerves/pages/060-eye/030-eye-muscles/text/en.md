Six muscles turn each eye, four straight recti and two obliques, and a seventh lifts the upper lid. Three cranial nerves work them. The [oculomotor nerve](@oculomotor-nerves) supplies [five of them](@oculomotor-muscles): the superior, inferior and medial recti, the inferior oblique and the lid lifter.

The thin [trochlear nerve](@trochlear-nerves), the only cranial nerve that leaves the back of the brainstem, supplies the [superior oblique](@superior-oblique-muscles), whose tendon runs through a pulley of cartilage. The [abducens nerve](@abducens-nerves) supplies the [lateral rectus](@lateral-rectus-muscles), which turns the eye outward. A palsy of any of the three throws the eyes out of line, and the person sees double.
