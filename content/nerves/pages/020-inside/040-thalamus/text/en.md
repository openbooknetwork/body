In the middle of the brain sit the two [thalami](@thalami), each about 3 centimetres long. Every sense except smell passes through a thalamus on its way to the cortex. The thalamus also relays the loops of the basal ganglia and the cerebellum back to the cortex.

@image thalamus-gray-62179d

Below them lies the [hypothalamus](@hypothalamus), less than 1 per cent of the brain. It holds the body at 37 degrees, drives hunger and thirst, sets the daily rhythm of sleep, and governs the [pituitary gland](@pituitary-gland) that hangs from it on a stalk. The pituitary, half a gram to a gram, controls the thyroid, adrenal glands and gonads with its hormones.
