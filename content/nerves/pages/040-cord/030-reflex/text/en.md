A tap just below the [kneecap](@patellae) stretches the tendon of the [quadriceps](@quadriceps), the muscle on the front of the thigh. Stretch sensors inside the muscle, the muscle spindles, fire. Their fibres run up the [femoral nerve](@femoral-nerves) to the cord, which they enter at the second to fourth lumbar segments.

@image lumbar-plexus-gray-507f92

There each sensory fibre ends directly on the motor neurons of the same muscle. After that one synapse the command runs straight back out, the quadriceps contracts and the leg kicks. With one tap a doctor tests the nerve, the cord segments and the motor neurons.

@image knee-jerk-reflex-arc-04a458

Wilhelm Erb and Carl Westphal both described the reflex in 1875, in the same issue of a Berlin journal.
