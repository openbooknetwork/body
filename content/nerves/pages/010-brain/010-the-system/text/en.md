The nervous system has two parts. The central part is the [brain](@brain) inside the skull and the [spinal cord](@spinal-cord) inside the spine. The peripheral part is the [nerves](@peripheral-nerves): 12 pairs leave the brain and 31 pairs leave the cord, and between them they reach every muscle, gland and patch of skin.

An adult brain weighs about 1.4 kilograms, some 2 per cent of the body, and holds about 86 billion nerve cells. At rest it uses about 15 per cent of the body's energy and receives 750 to 900 millilitres of blood a minute. When that flow stops, consciousness is lost within 5 to 10 seconds.
