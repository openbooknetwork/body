Five nerves serve the arm. The [musculocutaneous nerve](@musculocutaneous-nerves) works the biceps, and the [axillary nerve](@axillary-nerves) winds round the neck of the humerus to the deltoid. The [radial nerve](@radial-nerves) spirals round the back of the humerus to the muscles that straighten the elbow, wrist and fingers. Crushed against the bone, it leaves the wrist hanging.

The [median nerve](@median-nerves) runs down the forearm and through the carpal tunnel at the wrist, where swelling can squeeze it and numb the thumb, index and middle fingers. The [ulnar nerve](@ulnar-nerves) passes behind the bony point of the elbow, the funny bone, where a knock tingles the little finger, and works most of the small muscles of the hand.
