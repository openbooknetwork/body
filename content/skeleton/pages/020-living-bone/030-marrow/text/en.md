The spaces inside bones hold marrow. Red marrow makes the blood cells, more than two million red cells every second, along with the white cells and platelets. At birth all marrow is red. Through childhood the marrow in the shafts of the long bones turns into yellow marrow, which is mostly fat.

@image bone-marrow-gray-0a9854

In an adult, red marrow remains in the [skull](@cranium), the [vertebrae](@vertebral-column), the [ribs](@ribs), the [sternum](@sternum) and the [hip bones](@hip-bones), and in the upper ends of the femur and humerus. To test it, doctors draw a sample with a needle, most often from the back of the hip bone.

After heavy blood loss, yellow marrow can turn red again and make blood.
