Each hand and wrist has 27 bones in three groups. The eight [carpals](@right-carpals) form the wrist in two rows of four. The five [metacarpals](@right-metacarpals) make the palm. The 14 [phalanges](@right-finger-bones) make the fingers, two in the thumb and three in each of the others.

@image hand-bones-gray-4ee1cc

The two hands hold 54 bones, about a quarter of all the bones in the body. Their many small joints let the hand fit itself to what it holds.
