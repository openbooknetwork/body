An adult skeleton has 206 bones. It holds the body up, gives the muscles something to pull on and shields the brain, the heart and the lungs. In a man of 70 kg the skeleton, with the marrow and cartilage it holds, weighs about 10 kg.

@image skeleton-front-diagram-e4f160

The bones fall into two sets. The [axial skeleton](@axial-skeleton) runs down the middle of the body: the skull, the vertebral column, the ribs and the sternum, 80 bones in all. The [appendicular skeleton](@appendicular-skeleton) is the 126 bones of the arms and legs, with the girdles that join them to the trunk.
