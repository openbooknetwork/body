Anatomists sort bones by their shape. A long bone such as the [femur](@right-femur) is a shaft with a wider end at each side. The limbs are built of long bones, down to the small bones of the fingers and toes. Short bones such as the [carpals](@right-carpals) of the wrist are about as wide as they are long.

Flat bones are thin curved plates, such as the [parietal bones](@parietal-bones) of the skull roof, the sternum, the ribs and the shoulder blades. They shield organs and give muscles broad surfaces. Irregular bones fit neither group: a [vertebra](@third-lumbar-vertebra) is one.

Sesamoid bones grow inside a tendon where it runs over a joint. The [patella](@right-patella), the kneecap, is the largest of them.
