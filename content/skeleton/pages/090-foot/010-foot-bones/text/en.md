Each foot has 26 bones: 7 tarsals, 5 metatarsals and 14 phalanges. The [talus](@right-talus) sits on top, under the tibia and fibula, and passes the body's weight down and forward. No muscle attaches to it. Below and behind it lies the [calcaneus](@right-calcaneus), the heel bone, the largest bone of the foot.

In front of them lie the navicular, the cuboid and three cuneiforms, the [other tarsals](@right-midfoot-tarsals). The five [metatarsals](@right-metatarsals) make up the front of the foot, and their heads take the weight at the ball of the foot. The [phalanges](@right-toe-bones) of the toes match those of the fingers, two in the big toe and three in each of the others.

The two hands and the two feet together hold 106 bones, more than half of the 206.
