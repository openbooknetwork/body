The skull has 22 bones. Eight form the [cranium](@cranium), the case around the brain, and fourteen form the [face](@face). All but one are locked together along seams called sutures. The [mandible](@mandible), the lower jaw, is the only one that moves. A man's cranium holds about 1.4 litres.

The cranium's floor is pierced by holes for nerves and vessels. The largest, the foramen magnum, lets the spinal cord through to the brain.

Inside each [temporal bone](@temporal-bones) lie the three smallest bones of the body, the malleus, incus and stapes of the middle ear. They carry sound from the eardrum to the inner ear. The stapes is about 3 mm long. This model lacks them.
