The [mandible](@mandible) is the largest and strongest bone of the face, and it holds the lower teeth. At each end it rises to a rounded head, the condyle, which sits in a hollow of the temporal bone just in front of the ear. This is the temporomandibular joint, which you can feel move as you open your mouth.

@image mandible-gray-0e20da

A plate of fibrous cartilage, the [articular disc](@right-jaw-joint-disc), divides each joint into an upper and a lower space. When the mouth opens a little, the condyle turns like a hinge. To open wide, the condyles and discs slide forward onto a ridge of the temporal bone. If they slide past the ridge and lock there, the jaw is dislocated.

@image jaw-joint-section-gray-6405ca

At birth the mandible is two halves joined at the chin. They fuse into one bone during the first year.
