The [sternocleidomastoid](@sternocleidomastoid) runs up the side of the neck from the sternum and clavicle to the mastoid process behind the ear. One contracting alone tilts the head toward its own shoulder and turns the face to the other side. The two together bend the neck forward.

@image neck-side-gray-4f18ee

The [trapezius](@trapezius) is a flat sheet over the back of the neck and the upper back, from the skull and the spines of the vertebrae to the clavicle and the scapula. Its upper fibres shrug the shoulders and carry the weight of the arms. Its middle and lower fibres pull the shoulder blades together and down.

@image neck-muscles-openstax-8a8ed7

Both muscles are supplied by the accessory nerve, the eleventh cranial nerve. A doctor tests it by asking the patient to shrug against resistance and to turn the head against a hand.
