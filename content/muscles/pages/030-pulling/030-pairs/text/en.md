A muscle can only pull, so each movement has a muscle that undoes it. At the elbow the [biceps](@biceps-brachii) bends the joint and the [triceps](@triceps-brachii) on the back of the arm straightens it. The muscle that makes a movement is called the agonist, and the one that opposes it the antagonist.

@image antagonists-arm-81cbfd

The triceps has three heads. The long head starts on the scapula below the shoulder socket, the lateral and medial heads on the back of the humerus. All three end on the olecranon, the point of the elbow. The triceps straightens the elbow in pushing, throwing and getting up from a chair with the arms.

@image arm-forearm-muscles-openstax-6e924a

When the biceps contracts, nerve circuits in the spinal cord relax the triceps, and the other way round. The two can also work together at low force to hold the elbow steady while the hand does careful work.
